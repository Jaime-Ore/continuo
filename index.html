Continuo

Play guitar, and a band plays along: drums and bass that decide each 16th note before it happens. Five short videos follow the signal from the string to the speakers; the study lessons below them go into every part in depth, from the data to the models and the tests.

    A University of Melbourne engineering capstone, 2026. Team E-12-MJON-040: Jaime Orelowitz, Sena Fukushima, Ravindi Gamage, Felix Liu. Supervisor: Prof. Jonathan Manton.

    The study lessons

    Longer narrated lessons made for the team while writing the report: the data, how the models learn, the three architectures, and the battery of tests. Every number comes from the project's own code and runs.
